About

From notebook to production.

Shirsha Nag is a B.Tech CSE (IoT & Cybersecurity) student at the Future Institute of Technology, and an independent builder who loves shipping products people can actually use. He turns ML and LLM ideas into working systems, from time-series forecasting deployed with FastAPI and Streamlit to local LLM apps running on Ollama.

He is an IEEE peer reviewer for GCON'26 at IIT Guwahati, the founding president of FIT's first ACM Student Chapter, and has presented four papers at national and international conferences. Quantum ML is one of his research threads, alongside classical ML and applied AI. Latest ship: TaskPilot.
